AI Use in Education: The Good, the Bad, and the Wastes of Time

Generative AI, such as LLMs, has seen a meteoric rise in usage - especially since the advent of ChatGPT-3 in November 2022. Attendees of this conference will learn about the education system's response to language-model technology - the upsides, the downsides, and the pitfalls that students and educators encounter every day.
